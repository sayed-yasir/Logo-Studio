Modern Classic Logo Prompts

Modern classic logos keep the structure and proportion of traditional design but strip it back with modern restraint.

About the Modern Classic style

Typical cues include classical letterforms, symmetry and crest-like structure, with ornament removed and details cleaned up. The aim is to keep a sense of heritage without it feeling dated or theatrical.

The key decision is which classical cues to keep and which to drop. Keeping them all produces pastiche; dropping them all loses the character.

Brands it can suit

Restaurants, professional services, publishers, boutique hotels and heritage brands refreshing an older identity.

Design directions in the Modern Classic library

Six of the design ideas that Modern Classic prompts can start from. Each Modern Classic prompt in the library pairs one idea like these with its own composition, typography and color logic.

  • A simplified arch built from three flat planes.
  • A monogram-free roundel with a thin line and a bold dot.
  • A thin serif capital paired with a bold geometric disc.
  • A simplified fleur-free trefoil of three circles.
  • A classical grid of nine squares with one removed.
  • A balanced pair of vertical bars with an arch above.

Practical design considerations

  • Decide which classical cues stay, such as serifs, symmetry or a crest-like frame.
  • Remove ornament that does not add meaning.
  • Test in both digital and print contexts.
  • Avoid copying a historic style too literally.
